About Musical Bhailog

Musical Bhailog is a music and cultural platform celebrating artists, melodies, stage performances and the vibrant traditions of Mithila. Our aim is to bring listeners and performers together through songs, videos, photographs and live moments. This space will share our journey, team, musical work, events and future plans. We believe music connects people across places and generations.
